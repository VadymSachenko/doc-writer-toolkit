# Acronyms and abbreviations

- Use an acronym only when it saves meaningful repetition and the audience will recognize it.
- At first use, write the full term followed by the acronym in parentheses. Skip the
  expansion only for forms universally understood by the target audience.
- Do not introduce an acronym that appears only once or twice.
- Preserve the established capitalization and punctuation.
- Do not add an apostrophe to form a plural: *APIs*, not *API's*.
- Use the article that matches pronunciation: *an SDK*, *a URL*.
- Avoid an acronym in a title when readers may encounter the title before its definition.
- Reintroduce the full term when a long gap or standalone context could make the acronym unclear.
