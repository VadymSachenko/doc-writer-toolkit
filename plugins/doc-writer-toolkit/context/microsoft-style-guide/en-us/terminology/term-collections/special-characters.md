# Special characters

- At first instructional use, name an unfamiliar character and show it in parentheses.
- Preserve literal symbols in code, commands, formulas, paths, URLs, UI, and product names.
- Do not use `&`, `+`, `/`, `~`, or arrows as ordinary prose substitutes when words are clearer.
- Consider how a screen reader announces or skips the character.
- Distinguish hyphen, minus sign, en dash, em dash, slash, and backslash.
- Do not add typographic variants to code or text the reader must enter.
