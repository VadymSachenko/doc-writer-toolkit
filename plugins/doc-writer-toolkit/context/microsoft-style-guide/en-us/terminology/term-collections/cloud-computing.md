# Cloud-computing terms

- Use *cloud* only when the distributed, remotely delivered nature of the service matters.
- Hyphenate *cloud-based* before a noun; write *in the cloud* for location.
- Use *public cloud*, *private cloud*, *hybrid cloud*, and *multicloud* precisely.
- Write *on-premises* for software or infrastructure at a customer's site. Avoid
  *on-premise* and the abbreviation *on-prem* in formal documentation.
- Expand IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS at first use for audiences that may not know them.
- Distinguish a service, subscription, tenant, region, datacenter, resource, and instance.
- Do not use *the cloud* as a vague substitute for the named service or architecture.
