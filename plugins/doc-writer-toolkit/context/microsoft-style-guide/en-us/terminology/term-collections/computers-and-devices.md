# Computer and device terms

- Use the most specific ordinary term the audience needs: *computer*, *PC*, *device*,
  *desktop*, *laptop*, *tablet*, or *phone*.
- Do not call physical hardware a device when its established name is clearer.
- Distinguish *screen* or *display* from the physical *monitor*.
- Use *keyboard*, *mouse*, *touchpad*, *pen*, and *controller* for the actual input method.
- Prefer *storage* or *storage space* for capacity. Use *drive* for the logical or physical
  drive and *disk* only when a physical disk is meant.
- Use *memory* for RAM, not as a general synonym for storage.
- Preserve official port, connector, standard, and product names.
