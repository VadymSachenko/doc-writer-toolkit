<a id="assistive"></a>
## assistive

- Prefer to describe devices, technologies, and organizations that help people with disabilities.
- Eye trackers and motion sensors are examples.
- Keep in mind that some devices and organizations are useful to everyone.
- Describe them as assistive only in discussions about supporting people with disabilities.
- Source: [Microsoft Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/a-z-word-list-term-collections/a/assistive)

<a id="assure-ensure-insure"></a>
## assure, ensure, insure

- Ensure, insure, and assure are interchangeable in many situations.
- To improve worldwide readability, Microsoft style makes these distinctions:
- Prefer ensure to mean to make sure or to guarantee. - Use insure to mean to provide insurance. - Use assure to mean to state positively or to make confident.
- Source: [Microsoft Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/a-z-word-list-term-collections/a/assure-ensure-insure)

## author

## auto-

- In general, don’t hyphenate words beginning with auto-, such as autoscale and autodial, unless it's necessary to avoid confusion.
- When in doubt, check The American Heritage Dictionary.
