<a id="2d-two-dimensional"></a>
## 2D, two-dimensional

- Write in full on the first mention if your audience may be unfamiliar with the term.
- On subsequent mentions, use 2D.
- Write with a hyphen two-dimensional.
- Source: [Microsoft Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/a-z-word-list-term-collections/numbers-symbols/2d-two-dimensional)

<a id="3d-three-dimensional"></a>
## 3D, three-dimensional

- Prefer 3D most of the time.
- Write in full three-dimensional on the first mention if your audience may be unfamiliar with the acronym.
- Write with a hyphen three-dimensional.
- Source: [Microsoft Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/a-z-word-list-term-collections/numbers-symbols/3d-three-dimensional)

<a id="8-5-11-paper"></a>
## 8.5" × 11" paper

- Prefer instead of 8.5-by-11-inch, 8.5 × 11-inch, 8 1/2 by 11-inch, or other ways of referring to the paper size.
- Prefer a straight quotation mark (") to signify inches.
- Prefer a multiplication sign (×), not the letter x.
- Source: [Microsoft Style Guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/a-z-word-list-term-collections/numbers-symbols/85-x-11-paper)
