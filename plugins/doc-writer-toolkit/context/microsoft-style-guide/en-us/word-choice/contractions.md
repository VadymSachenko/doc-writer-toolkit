# Contractions

- Use common contractions to create a natural, friendly English voice.
- Do not force a contraction that sounds awkward, ambiguous, or too casual for the context.
- Avoid contractions in legal text, exact quotations, or other contexts requiring the
  uncontracted form.
- Be careful with negative contractions in safety-critical statements; use the form that
  makes the prohibition unmistakable.

**Recommended:** You can't restore the key after you delete it.  
**Acceptable when emphasis is needed:** You cannot restore the key after you delete it.
