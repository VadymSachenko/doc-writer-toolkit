# Em dashes, en dashes, hyphens, and minus signs

- Use an em dash—without surrounding spaces—to set off an abrupt explanation or break.
  Use it sparingly; parentheses, commas, or two sentences may be clearer.
- Use an en dash for compact numeric ranges such as pages or years. Do not combine
  *from* with an en-dash range.
- Use a minus sign for subtraction and negative numbers.
- Use a hyphen in established compound modifiers before a noun when needed for clarity.
- Do not hyphenate an adverb ending in `-ly` with the adjective it modifies.
- Omit a temporary compound hyphen when the modifier follows the noun unless the
  established term keeps it.
- Add a prefix hyphen when needed to avoid a misleading spelling or collision with
  another word.
- Preserve product names, trademarks, code, URLs, and UI spellings even when they differ.
- Never use a hyphen as a dash or minus sign.
