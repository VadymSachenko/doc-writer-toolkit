# Commas and colons

## Commas

- Use the serial comma before the final conjunction in a series of three or more.
- Use commas to separate introductory material, nonrestrictive information, direct
  address, and independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction.
- Do not separate a subject from its verb or a verb from its object.
- If a sentence needs many commas, split or restructure it.

## Colons

- Prefer a direct rewrite over a colon that merely amplifies the preceding statement.
- In ordinary body text, lowercase the first word after a colon unless it is a proper
  noun or begins a direct quotation.
- Within an English title, capitalize the first word after a colon.
- Do not put a colon at the end of a title, heading, UI label, image introduction, table
  introduction, or code-sample introduction. Use a period for the latter three.
- Before a list, use a colon after a complete sentence only when it explicitly points to
  the list. Use a colon after an introductory fragment only when the fragment combines
  grammatically with the items.
- Omit a UI label's trailing colon when referring to it in documentation.
