# Content and design planning

## Before writing

Define:

- The specific audience, its existing knowledge, and its vocabulary.
- The reader's goal, urgency, context, and likely obstacles.
- The business purpose and measurable success criteria.
- The best content type for the task, not merely the easiest format to produce.
- How readers will find, access, navigate, translate, and reuse the content.
- Expected devices, viewport sizes, accessibility needs, budget, and schedule.

## Design rules

- Involve design and localization partners before the structure is fixed.
- Start from an approved template and use its semantic styles.
- Use few styles and apply each one consistently.
- Avoid manual formatting that duplicates a template style.
- Keep recurring elements in predictable locations.
- Preserve white space; do not fill every available area with text or decoration.
- Plan text length, images, tables, and responsive behavior together.

## Final review

1. Recheck the audience goal, value, key message, and next action.
2. Ask a reviewer who was not involved in drafting to test comprehension.
3. Read the text aloud for voice, rhythm, missing words, and complexity.
4. Read only headings, then only opening sentences; they should form a coherent outline.
5. Verify customer search terms in the opening, titles, and headings.
6. Remove material that does not change understanding or action.
7. Check spelling, grammar, formatting, links, examples, code, and visual assets.
8. Confirm the final voice is warm, clear, concise, and helpful.
