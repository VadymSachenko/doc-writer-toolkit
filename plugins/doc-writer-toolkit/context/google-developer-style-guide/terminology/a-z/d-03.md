## `dummy variable`

**Scope:** `do-not-use`  
**Rule:** Do not use to refer to placeholders. Prefer placeholder. Also Do not use if referring to the concept in statistics known as a dummy variable. Prefer alternate terms such as indicator variable, design variable, one-hot encoding, Boolean indicator, binary variable, or qualitative variable.

[Official entry](https://developers.google.com/style/word-list#dummy-variable)
