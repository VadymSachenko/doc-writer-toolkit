# J terminology — 1 of 1

Scope labels are cumulative. `general` means no product-only marker was

<a id="term-jank"></a>
## `jank, janky`

**Scope:** `use-with-caution`  
**Rule:** Use only to refer to a glitch or problem with graphics that is caused by a loss of data or inadequate refresh rate. Do not use otherwise. Use a less figurative term to refer to something of poor or unreliable quality.

[Official entry](https://developers.google.com/style/word-list#jank)

<a id="term-just"></a>
## `just`

**Scope:** `use-with-caution`  
**Rule:** Avoid. If your meaning is unclear without just, then use a more specific term such as only, instead, or previously, or revise your language to be more specific. (Even if one of these replacement terms fits, you often Do not need it.) Sometimes, just is useful for conveying that one approach is simpler than another. In those cases, use just instead of simply.

[Official entry](https://developers.google.com/style/word-list#just)
