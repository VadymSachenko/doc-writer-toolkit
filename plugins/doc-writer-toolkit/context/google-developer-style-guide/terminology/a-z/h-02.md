# H terminology — 2 of 2

Scope labels are cumulative. `general` means no product-only marker was
present; a project-specific rule can still override this entry.

<a id="term-housekeeping"></a>
## `housekeeping, house keeping, house-keeping`

**Scope:** `do-not-use`  
**Rule:** Do not use. Prefer less figurative and more precise terms, such as maintenance and cleanup.

[Official entry](https://developers.google.com/style/word-list#housekeeping)
