# Dashes, hyphens, and ranges

## Dashes

- Use an em dash—without surrounding spaces—for a strong interruption or an emphasized
  explanation.
- Do not use a hyphen or en dash where an em dash is required.
- Google style rarely uses the en dash. Rewrite a relationship or range instead.

## GDSG-HYPHEN-001 — Follow the compound hierarchy

For a compound term, apply this order: project convention, the local Google word list,
then the first applicable Merriam-Webster form.

- Most prefixes are closed: **preconfigured**. Use a hyphen with **self-**, **cross-**,
  before a capital or number, to prevent ambiguity, or when the base is already hyphenated.
- Hyphenate a compound modifier before a noun when needed for clarity: **high-availability
  cluster**. Avoid long stacks of modifiers.
- Do not hyphenate an **-ly** adverb plus adjective: **highly available service**.
- A compound after the verb is usually open unless it is an established always-hyphenated
  term.

## GDSG-HYPHEN-002 — Format number and unit compounds

- Hyphenate a spelled-out number-plus-unit modifier before a noun: **a five-minute wait**.
- With an abbreviated unit, use a nonbreaking space and no hyphen: **a 5&nbsp;GB file**.

## GDSG-RANGE-001 — Use a hyphen for a range

Use a hyphen with no spaces between endpoints: **10-20 requests**. Do not mix a range
hyphen with **from** or **between**.

**Recommended:** from 10 to 20 requests; 10-20 requests  
**Not recommended:** from 10-20 requests

Use a suspended hyphen only when the shared term is clear, such as **two- and three-node
clusters**.
