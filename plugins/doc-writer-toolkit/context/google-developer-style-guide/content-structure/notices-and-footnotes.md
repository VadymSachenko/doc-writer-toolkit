# Notices and footnotes

## Notices

Use notices sparingly and choose the lowest accurate severity:

- **Note:** relevant noncritical context, exception, or tip.
- **Caution:** the reader must proceed carefully to avoid an unwanted result.
- **Warning:** risk of harm, data loss, security exposure, or another serious or
  irreversible consequence.
- **Success:** positive confirmation in an interactive flow; do not use as ordinary
  article decoration.

Put a notice immediately before the content or action it governs. State the consequence
and the safe action directly. Do not use several notices where ordinary prose or a
prerequisite section would be clearer.

## Footnotes

Avoid footnotes in developer documentation because they interrupt reading, localize
poorly, and can create accessibility problems. Put relevant information inline, in a
notice, or in a linked section. Use a footnote only when the publishing context requires
scholarly or legal citation behavior and provides accessible markup.
