# Accessible color, graphics, and media

## Rules

- Never communicate a distinction with color alone; add text, shape, pattern, icon,
  position, or another redundant cue.
- Do not hard-code colors that become unreadable in high-contrast themes.
- Use a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for ordinary text and its background.
- Avoid decorative backgrounds, watermarks, screens, or tints that reduce text contrast.
- Keep layouts simple and predictable; preserve a clear reading and tab order.
- Provide concise, useful alt text for informative non-text content.
- Give complex visuals an adjacent explanation or a linked long description.
- Do not duplicate nearby text in alt text or describe purely decorative images.
- Provide captions for meaningful audio, transcripts when appropriate, and audio or
  text descriptions for essential visual information in video.
- Make the written explanation sufficient when an image cannot be perceived.
- Provide text alternatives to image-map links and support keyboard navigation.
